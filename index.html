Legal framework
What's allowed.
What's not.

✅ Always OK Your own images

Removing watermarks, overlays, or text from photos or artwork you created yourself is fully legal in all jurisdictions. Pixelle is designed first for this use case.

✅ OK with licence Licensed stock images

If you have paid for and received a full-use licence to a stock image (e.g. from Shutterstock, Getty, Adobe Stock), removing the watermark version's overlay and using the licensed copy is legitimate. Always use the licence-granted file, not the preview.

✅ Always OK Timestamp & camera overlays

Removing date stamps, camera watermarks, GPS coordinates, and channel bugs from your own photos and recordings is perfectly legal.

⚠️ Not permitted Third-party copyright

Using Pixelle to strip watermarks from stock images you have not licensed in order to use them commercially is a violation of the DMCA (Section 1202) and equivalent laws globally. Our Terms of Service prohibit this use. Users who violate this policy are subject to account termination.

📋 By using Pixelle You confirm ownership or licence

At upload, users confirm they own or are licensed to edit the image. This user-generated confirmation is a standard legal mechanism that shifts liability appropriately and is used by virtually all professional image editing services.

FAQ
Common questions.
Is it legal to use Pixelle?
Yes — when used on images you own or have a valid licence for. Pixelle is a tool like Photoshop; legality depends on what you use it for, not the tool itself. See the Legal section above for a full breakdown.
Are my images uploaded to your servers?
On the Free tier, all processing happens entirely in your browser using the Canvas API — nothing is uploaded. On Pro and Team plans, images are sent to our servers for AI inpainting, processed immediately, and deleted within 30 minutes. We never store your images.
What's the difference between Smart Erase and AI Fill?
Smart Erase uses a pixel-sampling algorithm that reads surrounding colours and blends them inward — it's fast, local, and works great on simple backgrounds. AI Fill sends a patch to our inpainting model which understands context, texture, and lighting — it works far better on complex scenes like grass, fabric, or skin.
Do credits expire?
Never. Credits you purchase are yours to use at any pace. There's no monthly reset or expiry date — buy once, use whenever.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, any time, from your account dashboard with one click. You keep access until the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees, no dark patterns.
Do you offer refunds?
We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on monthly Pro plans, no questions asked. Annual plans get a 14-day guarantee. Credit packs are non-refundable once used but we're always happy to help if something went wrong.
